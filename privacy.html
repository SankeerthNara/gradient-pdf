Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version

Your PDFs never leave your device. Gradient PDF opens, previews and recolours files entirely inside your browser. Nothing you choose is uploaded to us or anyone else.

Your files and passwords

When you pick, drop or paste a PDF, your browser reads it into memory on your device. Recolouring and the downloaded copy are produced there too. If a PDF is password-protected, the password you type is used only in your browser to open the file and to lock the downloaded copy again. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

What is saved on your device

  • Theme (light or dark), so the site remembers your choice.
  • Saved custom styles (two slider positions each), so they appear next time.
  • Opening animation seen, for the current tab only, so it plays once per visit.

These live in your browser's own storage. We can't see them, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Your cookie choice is stored the same way.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request information such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics only loads after you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline, it never loads.

When accepted, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* to tell visits apart. It records things like pages viewed, referring site, approximate location, browser and device. Google processes this data on our behalf and may transfer it outside your country. It never sees your PDFs, passwords or styles.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Declining after accepting stops Google Analytics and removes its cookies from this site.

Children

The site isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
